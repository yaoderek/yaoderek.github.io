[Little City](https://littlecity.io/) is a cozy town-building game I made, drawn in ink and pencil. Play it free at [littlecity.io](https://littlecity.io/). It runs in any browser on a computer, iPhone, iPad or Android phone, with no download, no account and no ads.

It's the calm part of a city builder and nothing else. No money, no goals, no tycoon, and nothing can go wrong. It's closer to building a model town than managing one.

You place simple pieces (house, tower, lighthouse, arch, dock, mill, path, garden, wall) and the town works out what they become from where they are. A house beside a tall tower becomes a chapel, a house opening onto a square becomes a shop, four blocks stacked by the sea become a lighthouse. There are over 80 special buildings and events to find, all listed in [the building guide](https://littlecity.io/guide/).

The town lives on its own: residents walk to work, flour goes from the windmill to the bakery to the market stall, fishing boats go out, washing hangs on the lines, and there are market days, weddings, snow days, seasons, weather, and day and night. "Grow a town" grows a whole island by itself as a harbour town, castle town, market town, river town, hill town and more. Towns save in your browser and can be shared as a link or a postcard.

More on how it plays: [About Little City](https://littlecity.io/about/).
